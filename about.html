Your Instructor

Qualified. Experienced. Patient.

As the DVSA-approved ADI behind GIGADRIVE, I've been teaching Gravesend and surrounding areas drivers to pass with confidence for years. Every pupil is different — I tailor my style to suit you, whether you need calm encouragement or a push to tackle your weak spots.

I drive a dual-controlled car, registered G16 DRV, which you'll often spot on the A2, the Bluewater roundabouts and local test routes. My goal is simple: to get you to a standard well above what's needed to pass — so you're safe long after the test is over.

Our Story

Why We Started GIGADRIVE

GIGADRIVE started with a simple frustration: too many learners were passing tests without genuinely feeling confident on the road. And too many were failing because their lessons were rigid, hurried or impersonal.

We set out to do things differently. Every lesson at GIGADRIVE is built around you — your starting point, your nerves, your goals. We use a structured but flexible approach inspired by the DVSA's own learning framework, so progress is clear and measurable throughout.

The name GIGADRIVE is our promise: massive energy, massive drive, and a massive commitment to getting you where you need to be — behind the wheel, independently, safely.

Our Approach

How We Teach

A clear, structured journey from first lesson to first-time pass.

01

Assessment

We start with a brief assessment drive (or a beginner intro) so we understand your exact starting point and can tailor your lesson plan accordingly.

02

Structured Learning

Each lesson targets specific skills — from clutch control to roundabouts to independent driving. You'll always know what you're working on and why.

03

Mock Tests

Before your real test, we run full mock examinations on actual Gravesend and surrounding areas test routes so the real thing feels familiar, not frightening.

04

Test Day & Beyond

We help you book your practical test and can take you there in our car.
