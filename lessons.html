Refresher Lessons

Haven't driven in years? Maybe you passed your test a long time ago but life got in the way. Or perhaps you moved to the UK and are adapting to left-hand traffic. Refresher lessons rebuild your confidence fast.

We start with a short assessment drive so we can pinpoint exactly what needs work — no time wasted on skills you already have.

Common refresher topics

  • Motorway and dual carriageway driving
  • Busy urban driving and roundabouts
  • Parallel parking and manoeuvres
  • Night driving and adverse conditions
  • Adapting to a new area's road layout
  • Restoring confidence after an accident or break

Know Before You Go

Theory Test Support

Before you can sit your practical driving test, you'll need to pass the DVSA theory test — two parts: a multiple-choice section and a hazard perception video test.

We point you to the best official resources and practice apps, help you understand what to expect, and can advise on when to book your theory test relative to your practical progress.

We'll help you with

  • Understanding the theory test format
  • Best free and paid practice resources
  • Hazard perception tips from real lessons
  • When to book your theory test
  • Translating theory into real-world driving
