Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

GIGADRIVE is a DVSA-approved driving school based in Gravesend and surrounding areas, UK. We operate the website gigadrive.co.uk (the "Site"). Our registered vehicle is G16 DRV.

For any data-related questions, please contact us at hello@gigadrive.co.uk.

What Personal Data We Collect

When you use our contact or booking form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your general location (town or area)
  • Your message / enquiry content
  • Your lesson type preference

We also collect standard website analytics data (page views, device type, browser, approximate location) through cookies — see our Cookie Policy below.

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or booking request
  • Schedule and confirm driving lessons
  • Send lesson reminders (where you have requested this)
  • Improve the Site and our services using aggregated, anonymised analytics

We will never sell your personal data to third parties. We will never share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contractual necessity — to fulfil the booking and delivery of driving lessons
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to your enquiries and improve our services
  • Consent — where you have ticked the consent checkbox on our contact form

Data Retention

We retain your contact information for up to 2 years after our last interaction, after which it is securely deleted. Lesson booking records may be retained for up to 6 years for accounting and legal purposes.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where consent was the legal basis)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@gigadrive.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Third-Party Services

Our Site may use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (fonts loaded from Google servers)
  • Google Maps — if embedded on the Contact page
  • Formspree / EmailJS — for form submission handling (if configured)
  • Social media platforms — links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X

Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work efficiently and provide information to site owners.

Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
giga_cookie_notice Functional Remembers you've dismissed our cookie notice 1 year
_ga, _ga_* Analytics Google Analytics (if configured) — measures site traffic 2 years

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or clear cookies when you close your browser.

Note that disabling functional cookies may affect the way this Site works.
